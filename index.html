Our Story

Where vegetarian
becomes an art form

Bellora was born of a simple conviction — that vegetarian cuisine, treated with the reverence of haute gastronomy, can rival the finest tables in the world. In a sun-washed dining room of arches, cane and champagne light, our kitchen composes produce-driven plates that celebrate the depth of the Indian larder through a contemporary, elegant lens.

Every detail — from the hand-finished interiors to the quiet choreography of service — is composed to make an evening at Bellora feel less like dinner, and more like a memory in the making.
